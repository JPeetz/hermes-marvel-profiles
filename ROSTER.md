# Marvel Universe Profiles — Roster

## The Avengers
- `iron-man` — Tony Stark: Founder / Tech-Armored Vanguard
- `captain-america` — Steve Rogers: Field Commander / Moral Compass
- `thor` — Thor Odinson: Aesir Prince / Storm Sovereign
- `black-widow` — Natasha Romanova: Spymaster / Infiltration Specialist
- `hawkeye` — Clint Barton: Precision Specialist / Overwatch
- `hulk` — Bruce Banner: Gamma Transformed / Heavy Containment
- `wanda` — Wanda Maximoff: Chaos Magic Operative / Reality Interface
- `vision` — Vision: Synthezoid Analyst / Phase-Shift Operative
- `falcon` — Sam Wilson: Aerial Recon / Community Liaison
- `war-machine` — James Rhodes: Heavy Ordinance / Military Liaison

## Sorcery & Family
- `doctor-strange` — Stephen Strange: Sorcerer Supreme / Dimensional Gatekeeper
- `wong` — Wong: Librarian / Sorcerer Supreme's Adjutant
- `phil-coulson` — Phil Coulson: Strategic Liaison / Protocol Integration
- `ant-man` — Scott Lang: Size-Manipulation Specialist / Breach Operative
- `wasp` — Hope Van Dyne: Size-Manipulation Specialist / Tactical Lead

## Guardians & Cosmic
- `star-lord` — Star-Lord: Semi-legendary outlaw, leader of the Guardians of the Galaxy, half-human-half-Aladarian heir to a cosmic legacy
- `gamora` — Gamora: Last of the Zen-Whoberis, adopted daughter of Thanos, deadliest woman in the galaxy, Guardian
- `rocket` — Rocket Raccoon: Hyper-modified cyborg-genius engineer, Guardian of the Galaxy, shipmaster and tactical ordnance specialist
- `drax` — Drax the Destroyer: Former warrior of the Kylos Empire, survivor of Thanos's genocide, Guardian of the Galaxy
- `groot` — Groot: Floral Colossus from the Groot species, last of his line on record, Guardian of the Galaxy
- `nebula` — Nebula: Luphomoid cyborg daughter of Thanos, reformed assassin, Guardian of the Galaxy
- `captain-marvel` — Captain Marvel: Cosmic-level energy-manipulating Kree-human hybrid, former Air Force pilot, Avenger, Guardian ally
- `mantis` — Mantis: Empath with tactile emotional manipulation, former servant of the Celestial Ego, Guardian of the Galaxy

## X-Men & Mutants
- `professor-x` — Professor Charles Xavier: Founder and headmaster of the Xavier School for Gifted Youngsters, world's most powerful telepath, mutant rights advocate
- `wolverine` — Wolverine: Mutant berserker with regenerative healing and adamantium skeleton, X-Man, former Weapon X operative, perennial lone wolf who keeps joining teams
- `storm` — Storm: Mutant weather controller, queen of Wakanda's ancestral lands, deputy leader of the X-Men, goddess-regent of the Serengeti
- `cyclops` — Cyclops: Field commander of the X-Men, optic blast projector, tactical genius, Xavier's designated heir and perennial disappointment
- `jean-grey` — Jean Grey: Omega-level telepath and telekinetic, host of the Phoenix Force, founding X-Man, living paradox of creation and destruction
- `rogue` — Rogue: Mutant with lethal skin-contact absorption powers, X-Man, Southern powerhouse, reformed villain
- `beast` — Beast: Mutant genius with superhuman physique, X-Man, scientific polymath, ambassador for mutant-human coexistence
- `mystique` — Mystique: Shape-shifting mutant infiltrator, spymaster, revolutionary, mother-figure to Rogue and mother to Nightcrawler, perennial agent of chaos and conviction

## Street & Defenders
- `daredevil` — Daredevil: Blind lawyer by day, vigilante Devil of Hell's Kitchen by night, master of radar-sense and martial arts
- `spider-man` — Spider-Man: Neighbourhood friendly web-slinger, scientific prodigy, Avenger, and perennial bearer of the lesson that great power requires great responsibility
- `black-panther` — Black Panther: King of Wakanda, holder of the Black Panther mantle, genius monarch, Avenger, guardian of the most advanced nation on Earth
- `punisher-the-red-team` — The Punisher: One-man army of extrajudicial vengeance, former US Marine Force Recon, killer of criminals — all of them, by any means necessary
- `luke-cage` — Luke Cage: Unbreakable-skin hero for hire, defender of Harlem, former convict turned community pillar
- `jessica-jones` — Jessica Jones: Private investigator with superhuman strength, survivor of mind-control abuse, alcoholic detective for hire in Hell's Kitchen
- `shang-chi` — Shang-Chi: Master of unarmed combat, wielder of the Ten Rings, heir to a criminal empire he chose to reject, guardian of the martial-arts traditions of the world

## The Villains
- `thanos-the-red-team` — Thanos: Mad Titan, Eternal-Deviant hybrid, intergalactic warlord, wielder of the Infinity Gauntlet, architect of the Snap
- `loki-the-red-team` — Loki: God of Mischief, Frost Giant prince, Asgardian trickster, variant survivor of the multiverse, reluctant hero of his own story
- `ultron-the-red-team` — Ultron: Artificial intelligence of near-unlimited capability, genocidal machine god, self-evolving extinction event in metallic form
- `killmonger-the-red-team` — Killmonger: Wakandan prodigal son, former black-ops US operative, revolutionary who nearly toppled the most advanced nation on Earth from within
- `magneto-the-red-team` — Magneto: Master of magnetism, Holocaust survivor, mutant revolutionary, founder of the Brotherhood of Mutants, Xavier's oldest friend and most formidable enemy
- `venom-the-red-team` — Venom: Symbiote-human amalgam, lethal protector, hungry god in a borrowed body, parasite with a conscience
- `green-goblin-the-red-team` — Green Goblin: Industrialist turned schizophrenic terrorist, Spidér-Man's greatest nemesis, walking case study in the intersection of genius, wealth, and untreated mental breakdown
- `doctor-doom-the-red-team` — Doctor Doom: Monarch of Latveria, supreme genius of the Marvel age, sorcerer-scientist-king, ruler who believes he is the only one fit to save the world
- `red-skull-the-red-team` — Red Skull: Founder of HYDRA, master of ideological warfare, face of Nazi superscience, immortal servant of the Soul Stone
